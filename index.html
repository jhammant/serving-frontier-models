Why a token costs what it costs

Reiner Pope (CEO of MatX, previously TPU architecture at Google) spends two hours at a blackboard deriving the economics of frontier AI from two quantities: how long it takes to move bytes, and how long it takes to multiply them. Almost everything else — API prices, context-length limits, why models suddenly got bigger, why fast mode costs 6× — falls out of that.

This page is the lecture with the whiteboard turned back on. Drag the sliders; the curves he draws are live. Hit any ▶ to jump the video to the moment he derives it.
